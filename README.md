# Data2ActionProject_Split-Liver-Transplantation-Allocation
Replication of Tang et al.’s learning-curve bandit (L-UCB) for split-liver allocation. Compares Standard UCB and L-UCB on UPMC, AGH and VAPHS, with sensitivity and robustness checks, plus an EDA of STAR transplant data. CMU Heinz, From Data to Action.
